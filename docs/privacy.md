# Glide — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 August 2026_

Glide is a personal planning and reflection app. It has no account and
no server. This policy describes what it does with what you put into
it, and it is short because the app collects nothing.

## What Glide collects

**Nothing.** No analytics, no telemetry, no crash-reporting service, no
advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs that phone home. Glide
does not know who you are and has no way to find out.

## What Glide stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on your device: rankings, goals,
tasks, grades, journal entries, photos, and contentment check-ins. It
lives in a database inside the app's own storage, which no other app
can read.

When something breaks, the technical details are written down on the
device too: the time, the error and the code it came from, the screen
you were on, the app version and build, and your iOS version and phone
model — for example `ios 18.5 · iPhone 13`. Anything that looks like
something you typed is stripped out before the entry is written.

**Settings → Problem log** shows you that text in full, exactly as it
would be sent. It only goes anywhere if you send it.

## Photos

If you attach a photo, Glide copies the one you chose from your photo
library into its own storage so it can show it beside that day's
record. It reads nothing else from your library, and the copy stays on
your device. Glide never asks for camera or microphone access.

## When information leaves your device

Only when you send it, and only where you send it. There are three
ways that can happen, and you start all three:

- **Send feedback** hands what you typed to your own mail app, addressed
  to glidefeedback@gmail.com, and you send it. Nothing is transmitted by
  the app itself. The problem log notes that you sent something — the
  timestamp and the topic — never what you wrote.
- **Send log** opens the share sheet with the problem log's text and you
  choose where it goes. The text is plain and visible before you send.
- **Backups** are encrypted with a passphrase you choose before they
  leave the app, and go wherever you decide to put them. Without that
  passphrase nobody can open one, including us.

Nothing in the app sends anything on its own, on a schedule, or in the
background.

## Notifications

Glide schedules reminders locally on your device. There is no push
server, so no notification is ever sent from anywhere else, and nothing
about your reminders leaves the phone.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Glide deletes its data with it, permanently. Export a backup
first from **Settings → Your data** if you want to keep it. Updating
the app preserves your data; deleting it does not.

**Settings → Reset everything** erases what Glide has stored and starts
you over, without deleting the app.

## Children

Glide is not directed at children and collects no information from
anyone.

## TestFlight

While Glide is distributed for testing through TestFlight, Apple
collects its own installation and crash data under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), separate
from anything described here. Glide neither controls nor receives it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
