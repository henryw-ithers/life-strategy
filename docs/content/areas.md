# Strategic Life Areas

The six areas, in taxonomy order. Each is a heading of the form
`## <area-id> — <Name>`, one paragraph saying what the area covers, and
a bulleted list of the units inside it in plain language.

Areas are **presentational** (ADR-0021): nothing here changes a weight,
a point value, or a stored row. This is the copy shown when someone taps
an area during onboarding or the diagnostic, and it exists because the
six colours are the first thing the app teaches and were, until now, the
only thing about an area a person could learn.

Voice follows [the copy guide](../design/copy-guide.md): plain, direct,
no reassurance. Say what the area is, not why it matters — a person
ranking their own life does not need to be told that health is
important.

## relationships — Relationships

The people you are close to, and the standing you have with them.

- Significant other: your romantic partnership, or your relationship with being single.
- Family: parents, siblings, children, chosen family.
- Friendship: the people you would call, and how much you actually do.

## physical-health — Physical health

The body you live in and what you ask of it.

- Exercise and fitness: moving on purpose, at whatever intensity is yours.
- Nutrition: what you eat, and how much thought goes into it.
- Sleep and recovery: hours, consistency, and whether you wake up restored.

## mental-wellbeing — Mental wellbeing

Your inner life: what you think about, and what you do with it.

- Mental and emotional health: mood, stress, and how you handle both.
- Spirituality: meaning, practice, or whatever you call the larger frame.
- Giving and service: what you do for people who are not close to you.

## work-money — Work and money

What you do for a living and what it pays for.

- Job and career: the work itself, and where it is heading.
- Learning and growth: skills you are building on purpose.
- Finances: what you earn, spend, owe, and keep.

## home-environment — Environment

The conditions you live in, and the upkeep that keeps them liveable.

- Living space: the state of where you live.
- Nature: time outdoors, in whatever form you get it.
- Hygiene: the daily maintenance nobody praises you for.

## leisure-creativity — Leisure and creativity

What you do when nothing is being asked of you.

- Hobbies and projects: the things you make or tinker with.
- Art and media: what you read, watch, and listen to on purpose.
- Adventure and experiences: travel, novelty, the days that stand out.
