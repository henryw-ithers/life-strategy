# Life Strategy — Support

Questions, problems or ideas: **henrywithersfeedback@gmail.com**.

The quickest way is from inside the app: **Settings → Send feedback**
writes the email for you, with the app's version filled in.

If something broke, **Settings → Problem log → Send log** shares the
technical details too. You see the text before it goes anywhere.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?** Only on your phone. Life Strategy has no account
and no server. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

**How do I keep my data when I change phones?** Settings → Your data →
Back up, then restore that file on the new phone. Deleting the app
deletes its data, so back up first.

**How is the daily score worked out?** Each day is out of 100 for how
much of that day's plan you got through. Tap the score on Home for the
breakdown.
